Simple workflow

From blank canvas to production report

Three steps to a fully documented LED wall build your crew can actually work from.

Features

Everything you need on show day

Built by people who have run cables in the dark. Every feature exists because someone needed it on a real gig.

📦

2,700+ Tile & Processor Library

Pre-built profiles from ROE Visual, Absen, Brompton, Novastar, and every major brand — rebuilt from manufacturer datasheets with corrected pixel dimensions, physical sizes, and power draw. Save and edit your own custom tiles and processors in a personal library that follows you across every project.

🧩

Modular Processor Support

NovaStar H Series chassis (H2 through H20) with mixed copper/fiber output cards, and the full COEX MX6000 Pro / MX2000 Pro card tree (Card ▸ CVT box ▸ ports) including the 5G solution. Give any card a custom name — it shows up everywhere that card is referenced, including the redundancy planner and PDF report.

🏗️

Multi-Wall Projects

Manage every wall at your event in one project file. Use the Breakaway Wall feature to slice a massive layout into individual tabs in one click. Switch walls instantly, with all other walls shown as faded outlines so nothing gets lost.

📐

Layout & Alignment Tools

Pixel-perfect coordinate inputs that work as calculators. Smart crossing selection, industry-standard alignment and distribution tools, grouping, tile locking, and bulk numbering in any direction. Rotate a whole selection as a rigid group with a single keystroke.

🔌

Accurate Port Capacity & Auto Router

Per-tile capacity math for NovaStar, Brompton, and HELIOS — receiver card class, closed-loop redundancy, 2.5G tiles, all modeled against each manufacturer's published tables. Right-click a port while routing to auto-fill it with a serpentine pattern instead of clicking every tile by hand.

⚡

Power Circuit Mapping

Add single circuits or full 6-circuit Socapex bundles, then organize them into named, nestable Circuit Groups with a live amp-budget rollup and dual 110V/208V load bars. A single circuit can span tiles across multiple walls, with Twofer branching and Edison / PowerCON / True1 connector tracking throughout.

🛡️

System Redundancy Planner

Click a processor to open it into a drill-down tree — output cards, then CVT boxes, then ports. Drag a whole card or CVT box onto another to back it up in one move, or drag a single port for a one-off failover line. Every backup pairing flows straight into the flow maps, technical drawings, and PDF report.

📄

Full Export Suite

One-click ZIP packages pixel maps, data and power flow diagrams (Dark, Light, or Schematic style), test cards, CSV and PDF production reports, a byte-accurate EDID binary for media servers, and an MP4 tearing-test video to spot frame-sync issues on-site. A live progress bar with ETA tracks every export.

✈️

Works Fully Offline

No internet required after activation. Auto-updates silently in the background when a new version is available — you decide when to install. Available in English, French, and Spanish. Your project files live on your machine, not a server.
